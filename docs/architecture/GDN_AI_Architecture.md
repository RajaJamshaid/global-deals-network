GDN AI Architecture

1. Purpose

GDN AI Architecture ka purpose centralized AI infrastructure define karna hai jo GDN ke Deals, Products, Search, Recommendations, Earn Engine, Merchant Marketplace, PSEO, Analytics aur User systems ko intelligent capabilities provide kare.

AI GDN ka separate business system nahi hoga.

AI existing centralized GDN systems ko consume aur enhance karega.

---

2. Core Principle

Central GDN Data + Specialized AI Services → Intelligent Global Commerce Experience

AI ko centralized APIs aur approved data sources ke through access milega.

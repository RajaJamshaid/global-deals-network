Global Deals Network

Order & Purchase Commerce Architecture

1. Purpose

GDN Order & Purchase Commerce Architecture defines how users move from discovering a product or deal to initiating, tracking, and completing a purchase.

Core flow:

User → Product → Offer → Price Comparison → Purchase Intent → Affiliate/Direct Merchant → Merchant Checkout → Purchase Attribution → Post-Purchase Support

---

2. Core Principle

GDN should not unnecessarily become the merchant's checkout system.

Where the merchant provides checkout:

GDN discovers and routes → Merchant completes transaction

Where GDN-owned commerce functionality is legally and technically applicable:

GDN may provide additional purchase functionality through approved payment and commerce services.

---

3. Central Commerce Layer

All purchase-related functionality must use one central Commerce Engine.

Channels must not create separate purchase logic.

Channels:

- Website
- Telegram Bot
- Telegram Mini App
- PWA

- Social channels

Central Commerce Engine

139. Core Commerce Rules

1. Product is canonical.
2. Offer represents merchant-specific commerce.
3. Active Market controls eligibility.
4. Affiliate links come only from Affiliate Engine.
5. Direct merchant fallback is supported.
6. Merchant checkout remains authoritative unless GDN owns checkout.
7. Purchase completion requires evidence.
8. Reward Wallet and Financial Ledger remain separate.
9. Commerce does not duplicate central engines.
10. Every financial/purchase event must be traceable.
